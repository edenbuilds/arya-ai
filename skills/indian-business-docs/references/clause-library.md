# Clause Library

Standard, plain-language clauses for common Indian documents. Adapt the bracketed parts.

---

## Universal clauses (most agreements need these)

**Governing law and jurisdiction**
> This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The courts
> at [City] shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

**Dispute resolution (arbitration)**
> Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably. Failing
> which, it shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and
> Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be [City] and the language English.

**Entire agreement**
> This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior
> discussions, representations, or agreements, whether oral or written.

**Notices**
> All notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses/email IDs mentioned above and shall be
> deemed received [X] days after dispatch.

**Severability**
> If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall
> continue in full force.

## Rent / Lease agreement

Essential clauses:
- Parties and property description
- Term (start date, duration) - note: 11-month agreements avoid mandatory registration in most states
- Rent amount, due date, mode of payment, escalation (typically 5-10% annually)
- Security deposit (typically 2-3 months in metros, up to 10 in some cities) and refund conditions
- Maintenance charges - who pays
- Permitted use (residential/commercial)
- Lock-in period
- Notice period for vacating (typically 1-2 months)
- Restrictions (subletting, structural changes, pets)
- Landlord's right to inspect (with notice)
- Conditions for deposit deductions

Flag: Stamp duty and registration vary by state. Agreements over 11 months usually need registration.

## NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)

Essential clauses:
- Definition of "Confidential Information" (be specific but broad)
- Obligations of receiving party
- Exclusions (already public, independently developed, required by law)
- Purpose limitation (info used only for the stated purpose)
- Term of confidentiality (survives agreement - typically 2-5 years, or perpetual for trade secrets)
- Return/destruction of materials on termination
- No license granted
- Remedies (injunctive relief, since damages alone don't fix a leak)

One-way vs mutual: Use mutual if both sides share sensitive info (most startup conversations).

## Employment offer letter

Essential clauses:
- Role, designation, reporting manager
- CTC breakdown (basic, HRA, allowances, variable, ESOP if any)
- Joining date and location
- Probation period (3-6 months) and confirmation
- Notice period (30-90 days)
- Working hours and leave policy reference
- Confidentiality and IP assignment (all work product belongs to employer)
- Non-solicitation (enforceable) - NOT blanket non-compete (Section 27 makes it unenforceable)
- Grounds for termination
- At-will vs cause-based termination

## Founder / Co-founder agreement

The document that prevents startup breakups from becoming lawsuits. Essential clauses:
- Equity split (exact percentages)
- Vesting schedule: standard is 4 years with 1-year cliff. If a founder leaves before 1 year, they
  get nothing; after that, monthly/quarterly vesting.
- Roles and responsibilities of each founder
- Decision-making (what needs unanimous consent vs majority)
- IP assignment - all IP created belongs to the company, not the individual
- Leaver provisions (good leaver vs bad leaver - what happens to unvested and vested equity)
- Founder salary/compensation
- Dispute resolution and deadlock breaking
- What happens if a founder wants to exit

Flag strongly: This is the single most important document for a startup. Recommend lawyer review.

---

## Freelance / Consulting agreement

Essential clauses:
- Scope of work (specific deliverables, not vague)
- Fee and payment schedule (advance, milestones, on completion)
- Payment timeline and late payment interest
- IP ownership - specify clearly. Default: client owns deliverables on full payment; contractor
  retains pre-existing IP and general know-how.
- Revisions included vs extra
- Termination and kill fee
- Independent contractor status (not employee - no PF/ESI)
- Confidentiality

## DPDPA 2023 Privacy Notice

Must cover (this is legally required for Indian data collection):
- Identity of the data fiduciary (your entity)
- Categories of personal data collected
- Purpose of collection and processing
- How long data is retained
- Whether data is shared with third parties (and who)
- User rights: access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, nomination
- Contact details of grievance officer / Data Protection Officer
- Consent mechanism and right to withdraw consent
- How to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board

Keep it readable. DPDPA emphasises clear, plain-language notices, not legalese walls.
