# Third-Party Materials

arya-ai combines and normalizes materials supplied to this repository by the user.

Included source licenses are preserved in `docs/source-licenses/`:

- `indian-law-plugin-LICENSE`
- `company-drafting-LICENSE`
- `indian-legal-drafting-LICENSE.txt`

The supplied `NCLT-Skills-main.zip` archive contained proprietary Anthropic license terms restricting extraction and redistribution. Those files are not redistributed in arya-ai. arya-ai includes an original `nclt-pdf-workflow` skill instead, covering local PDF and tribunal filing workflow practices without copying restricted source text.

- `skills/ai-practice-safeguards/` is original text. Its structure was informed by the free LLM Advocates course (learn.llmadvocates.com). No course text, prompts or templates are reproduced.
